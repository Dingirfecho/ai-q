§01 · Who it's for

Does your agency have these problems?

This solution applies directly if you manage properties in Argentina, have between 1 and 20 agents, and recognize your business in any of these scenarios:

Signs this solution is for you

  • You get inquiries at night or on weekends and there's no one to answer — the lead calls another agency
  • Your agents spend hours answering the same things over and over: price, size, HOA fees, availability
  • You coordinate visits over personal WhatsApp and end up with double bookings, no-shows or schedule conflicts
  • You have more than 5 agents and assigning properties by zone is a manual, error-prone process
  • You don't know how many inquiries you got yesterday, which channel they came from, or which property drew the most interest
  • You use a shared spreadsheet nobody updates in time, and leads get lost between chats
  • You want to offer search across Zonaprop, Argenprop and MercadoLibre without your team opening each portal
  • As the owner, you want to know how the day went without having to ask anyone
§02 · What it does

Five operational capabilities

[ 01 ]
Searches your own listings
The client asks, and the assistant queries your property Google Sheet in real time. It filters by type, area, price, rooms and status. It answers with the exact data from the listing — no invented facts, no improvising. If the data isn't there, it says so and offers direct contact.
[ 02 ]
Searches external portals
When the client asks for something you don't have in your own listings, the assistant runs parallel searches on Zonaprop, Argenprop and MercadoLibre. Gemini parses the results and replies with the most relevant options: price, area and a direct link to the original listing.
[ 03 ]
Schedules and assigns visits
The assistant detects visit intent, identifies the agent available for that area and notifies them via Telegram. The lead is logged in Sheets with status "Visit requested." The client gets confirmation on the same channel where they asked.
[ 04 ]
Answers technical questions
HOA fees, covered square footage, building age, floor, parking — available without bothering any agent. The assistant reads the full property listing and answers precisely. Average response time: under 15 seconds.
[ 05 ]
Reports to the owner every day
Every day at 8pm, Gemini generates an automatic executive summary: the day's inquiries by channel, most-viewed properties, scheduled visits, and leads needing urgent follow-up. Delivered by email and Telegram. No extra app to open.
[ ● ]
Memory across channels
If a client starts on Telegram on Monday, continues by email on Tuesday and wraps up on WhatsApp on Wednesday, the assistant remembers the whole conversation. History persists in a Data Store and is retrieved on every interaction, regardless of channel.
§03 · Architecture

How it works under the hood

The system is built on 5 Make.com scenarios that communicate with each other. Every incoming message triggers the full flow in under 30 seconds.

01
Channel receivers
Three separate scenarios listen on WhatsApp (Twilio webhook), Telegram (WatchUpdates) and Gmail (IMAP). Each one normalizes the incoming message into the same canonical format and passes it to the Central Agent.
02
Central Agent
Reads the conversation history from the Data Store. Gemini 2.5 Flash classifies the message's intent as: LISTING_QUERY, EXTERNAL_SEARCH, SCHEDULE_VISIT, PROPERTY_QUESTION or ESCALATE. It then runs the matching branch.
03
Response dispatcher
Receives the generated reply and sends it to the originating channel: Telegram, WhatsApp (Twilio) or Gmail. The lead is logged in Sheets. The agent gets their notification. History is updated in the Data Store.

§04 · External portals

Zonaprop, Argenprop and MercadoLibre — in real time

When a client asks about a property you don't have listed, the assistant doesn't say "we don't have that." It searches the three major portals and presents the most relevant options with price, area and a direct link to the listing.

The full process — from the client's message to a reply with results — takes under 20 seconds.

§09 · FAQ

What always comes up before deciding

FAQ · Real Estate AI Assistant
Does the client know they're talking to an AI?

Not necessarily. The assistant replies under the agency's name, in natural language. If the client asks to speak with a person, the system escalates immediately to the agent and notifies them on Telegram with the full conversation context.

What if the AI gives incorrect information?

The assistant answers using the data you load into Sheets — it doesn't invent or hallucinate. If the client asks something not on the listing, the system says so and offers to connect them with an agent. You can audit every conversation in the Data Store history.

Are my data and my clients' data secure?

The data lives in your Google Sheets, in your own Google account — not ours. Gemini processes the message text but doesn't store it or use it to train models. Make.com is GDPR and ISO 27001 compliant. You can audit what's stored at any time.

Does this replace my agents?

No. The assistant handles repetitive inquiries and filters leads. Agents receive pre-qualified prospects with full history and focus on negotiating and closing. With less time on WhatsApp, they're more productive.

Can I update the listings myself?

Yes, directly in the Google Sheet. Add a row, change a property's status to "Reserved" or update the price — the assistant uses the updated data on the next inquiry, with no code to touch and no one to call.
