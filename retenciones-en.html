●SOLUTIONSHEET 08 / WITHHOLDING TAX
§ Solution · Accounting & treasury

Automated withholding tax reconciliation by payment order/

"Every withholding read, checked and reconciled against its payment order."

Drop withholding certificates and payment order PDFs into a folder. The process extracts each withholding with AI, matches it against your customer master, chart of accounts and rate table, and checks that every payment order balances. Runs on Google Workspace or Microsoft 365.

§01 · The problem

Days lost every month

If your team opens each certificate, identifies the withholding agent, splits taxes line by line, looks up the ledger account, keys it in by hand and then checks against the payment order that invoices, payments and withholdings add up, it is losing 3 to 8 minutes per document. Multiplied by the monthly volume, that is days of administrative work you can recover.

+ 3 to 8 min per document

Not counting typos, wrong rates, payment orders that don’t balance, and time spent on accounting corrections.

§02 · The solution

How it works, in 6 steps

An automated process that takes PDFs as they arrive and returns them read, checked, reconciled and filed.

01
PDFs in a folder
Files go into a Google Drive or OneDrive folder. The process checks it every 10 minutes.
02
Reading and classification
Tells a standalone certificate apart from a full payment order with its annex. If a scan has no tax ID in its text, it reads it as an image.
03
AI extraction
Using Gemini or ChatGPT, splits out each withholding: tax, jurisdiction, taxable base, rate, amount and certificate number.
04
Cross-checks
Validates the tax ID (CUIT) against the customer master, assigns the ledger account and compares the rate with that month’s table.
05
Payment order reconciliation
Invoices − payments − withholdings must equal zero. If it doesn’t balance, the AI takes a second pass before flagging it.
06
Output and filing
Everything is written to Google Sheets or Excel, and each PDF is moved to Processed, Review or Rejected.

§04 · What’s included

A complete solution

◆
Reconciliation by payment order
Invoices, checks, transfers and annex withholdings each get their own sheet. Every payment order has its balance check, with a configurable peso tolerance.
◇
Full traceability
Every row carries a confidence score, a link to the original PDF and a processing timestamp. A per-file log ensures nothing is loaded twice.
⚠
Safeguards
When something doesn’t add up, it says so and explains why: low confidence, rate differs from the table, unbalanced payment order, or a conflict with one already loaded. It never overwrites data.
▲
Argentine rules built in
Recognizes all 24 gross income tax (IIBB) jurisdictions, monthly rates, jurisdictions that apply a Multilateral Agreement coefficient, and CUIT validation.
■
Your master data
Works with your chart of accounts, customer master and rate table. New tax IDs are added automatically for you to complete. Your own CUIT is fixed as the recipient.
●
Tracking dashboard
One screen showing what was processed, what needs review and what couldn’t be loaded. With users and roles, and sign-in with Google or Microsoft.
§05 · Why it works

Not a ChatGPT wrapper

This is not a generic OCR tool or a ChatGPT wrapper. It is a solution built specifically for Argentine withholdings and payment orders, with accounting and tax knowledge embedded in every check.

It runs on n8n or Make.com, with Gemini or ChatGPT as the AI engine, in the cloud or on your own server, and the data stays in your Google Drive or OneDrive. No lock-in: the workflow is visible and editable, and new versions install without touching what’s already loaded.
