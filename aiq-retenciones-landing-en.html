§02 · TWO FLOWS, ONE SYSTEM

It works for the withholdings you receive and the ones you have to issue.

RECEIVED

When a withholding comes in

The certificate your client emails you, or the file you download from the agency and drop in a folder. The system reads it, calculates the amount if it is not already calculated, audits it and builds the spreadsheet.

TO ISSUE

When you are a withholding agent

It calculates the amount using the rate that applies to the type of transaction and the supplier's status, and prepares the certificate ready to issue before you pay them.

§05 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you write to us.

Do I need my client to send me the withholding certificate?
Not necessarily. If the certificate arrives by email, we take it from there. If it does not, we still take the file you download from the agency (AFIP, ARBA, AGIP, etc.) from a folder. You do not depend on a single source.
What if the withholding is not calculated?
The system calculates it by applying the stated rate according to the current reference tables. It works whether the withholding already comes with the amount calculated or has to be calculated from scratch.
Does it also work if I (or my client) am a withholding agent?
Yes. In addition to processing the withholdings you receive, the system calculates the amount to withhold based on the type of transaction and the supplier's status, and prepares the certificate ready to issue before you pay them.
Does it replace my management or payroll/settlement system?
No. You keep settling with the same system; we hand you the file already built and audited, ready to upload to each jurisdiction.
What if the system makes a mistake?
Anything that does not validate with certainty is flagged and routed to someone on your team for review. Nothing is processed without review.
How much does it cost?
Fixed price based on your monthly volume — we put it together with your real data in the first conversation, with no never-ending project.
How long does it take to implement?
Between 2 and 15 days depending on volume and integration with your current system.
