§03 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you reach out.

Does it replace my current HR system?
No. It runs on top of the tools you already use for email, spreadsheets and files, plus the channels your team already has installed (email, WhatsApp, Telegram) — with no new software licenses and no servers to manage.
What if the notice is unclear or missing information?
Anything that cannot be identified with certainty is routed to an HR team member for review. Nothing is processed unchecked.
How much does it cost?
Fixed price, based on the modules you add (leave, new hires, monthly close, time bank, etc.) and your headcount. We'll work out the exact figure with you in the demo, using your real data.
How long does implementation take?
Between 2 and 6 weeks, including training for your HR team to run the solution.
