§02 · HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, without touching your current administration system.

01

The invoice arrives, or you drop it in the building's folder

The supplier emails it, or you upload it to that building's Drive folder — what you already do today, with no new steps.

02

The system identifies the supplier and calculates the withholding

An orchestrator connected to an LLM reads the CUIT and the line item, looks the supplier up in your registry, and applies the right rate — Ganancias (RG830), special SUSS for cleaning or security, and IIBB according to the building's jurisdiction.

03

Prepares the certificate and the file ready to upload to the agency

The withholding certificate is ready to issue before you pay the supplier, and the system also builds the file in the format each agency requires — ARCA (formerly AFIP/SICORE), ARBA, AGIP, as applicable — ready to file. The per-building record is kept as backup, not as the final destination. If a supplier is not in the registry, it is flagged so you can add it — nothing is calculated or filed blindly.

§04 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you write to us.

Do I have to enter each supplier by hand?
Yes, once per supplier: CUIT, VAT status, and, where applicable, the special SUSS regime (cleaning or security). From then on, every invoice from that supplier is processed automatically.
What happens if a new supplier is not in the registry?
The system detects it instantly, logs it in an exceptions sheet and notifies you by email — it never pays or calculates anything blindly. You add the supplier once and it runs on its own from there.
Does it work if I manage several buildings, each with its own account and IIBB jurisdiction?
Yes. Each building has its own folder, its own supplier registry and its own withholding record — the system never mixes them.
Is the data just logged in a spreadsheet, or can it be filed directly?
It can be filed directly. In addition to the per-building record (which serves as backup and reference), the system builds the file in the format each agency requires — ARCA (formerly AFIP/SICORE), ARBA, AGIP, or another depending on the jurisdiction — ready to upload. It is not just an internal-control Google Sheet or Excel file.
Does it replace my accounting firm or my administration system?
No. You keep settling building expenses and paying suppliers as always; the system hands you the certificate and the filing file already calculated and checked, ready to use.
What if the system applies the wrong rate?
Anything it cannot validate with certainty — supplier not in the registry, ambiguous regime, missing field — is flagged and routed for manual review. Nothing is processed unchecked.
How much does it cost?
Fixed price based on the number of buildings and monthly invoices — we put it together with your real data in our first conversation.
How long does implementation take?
Between 2 and 15 days, depending on how many buildings and suppliers need to be loaded into the initial registry.
